Firmware add-on · Canon IXUS 60 / PowerShot SD600

Film looks for old CCD cameras.

Press Print, flip through the film labels, pick a look, shoot. Every photo comes out of the camera finished – no editing, no app.

Choose your looks How it works

    1. Pick looks

      Choose the films you want on the card. The page builds one ZIP for you – right here in the browser.

    2. Unpack to the SD card

      EXCCD runs from the card. Nothing is written to the camera – take the card out and it is stock again.

    3. Press Print, shoot

      Each look sets Canon's colour engine and a tone curve on the raw data, so the JPEG is the finished picture.

    Firmware version

    How do I find my firmware version?
    1. Download vers.req and copy it to the root of the SD card.
    2. Put the card in the camera and switch it on in playback mode.
    3. Hold FUNC/SET and press DISP. The camera shows a line like Firmware Ver GM1.00D – the letter at the end is the one that counts.
    4. Delete vers.req from the card again if you like.
    02

    Looks

    Every look comes with two Dynamic+ variants (DR+1, DR+2): the camera exposes shorter to protect the highlights and the curve lifts the shadows. They are always included. The curve shows how each look maps tones; the line in the middle is neutral.

    Install

    1. Remove the old version

      Delete an old CHDK folder from the SD card, if there is one. An old FILMS.TXT can point to labels or curves that no longer exist.

    2. Unpack

      Unpack the ZIP to the root of the SD card, so that DISKBOOT.BIN and the CHDK folder sit at the top level.

    3. Start EXCCD

      Once per power-on: switch on in playback mode, open MENU → Firm Update and confirm.

      Automatically: make the card bootable (for example with STICK or CHDK's Make Card Bootable) and lock the card's write-protect switch. The camera still saves photos.

    4. Pick a look

      In shooting mode press Print, flip with the arrow keys, confirm with FUNC/SET. The active look is shown at the bottom of the screen.

    05

    Questions

    Can this damage my camera?

    EXCCD is based on CHDK, which has been used on Canon compacts since 2007. It runs from the SD card and does not change the camera's firmware. Without the card, the camera behaves exactly as before. As with any third-party software: use at your own risk.

    Do I lose anything compared to the normal camera?

    No. Choose Off in the film selection and the camera takes pictures with Canon's own colours. Canon's menus and modes stay as they are.

    How is this different from a filter app?

    The tone curve works on the sensor's raw data before the camera makes the JPEG, together with Canon's own colour settings. The look is part of the picture the camera saves – there is nothing to apply afterwards.

    Can I keep a raw file?

    Yes. CHDK can save a raw file (DNG) next to each JPEG. Turn it on in the options (MENU).

    Which look did I use for a photo?

    EXCCD writes every shot with its look to A/CHDK/FILMLOG.CSV on the card.

    Can I add looks later?

    Come back, pick a new set and replace the CHDK folder on the card with the new download.

    Why are the menus in German?

    EXCCD started as a personal project in Germany. The film labels are in English; only the option menus are German for now.

    What about other cameras?

    More CCD compacts are planned. Every new camera has to be bought, ported and tested – that is what your support pays for.
