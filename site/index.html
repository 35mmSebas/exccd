EXCCD

Film looks for old CCD cameras.

Pick the looks you want, download one ZIP, unpack it to the SD card. Press Print on the camera, choose a film, shoot. The pictures come out of the camera finished.

Runs from the SD card only, nothing is written to the camera. Free – pay what you want.

1Camera

Firmware version

How do I find my firmware version?
  1. Download vers.req and copy it to the root of the SD card.
  2. Put the card in the camera and switch it on in playback mode.
  3. Hold FUNC/SET and press DISP. The camera shows a line like Firmware Ver GM1.00D – the letter at the end is the one that counts.
  4. Delete vers.req from the card again if you like.

2Looks

Every look also has two Dynamic+ variants (DR+1, DR+2) that expose shorter to protect highlights and lift the shadows with the curve. They are always included.

4Install

  1. Delete an old CHDK folder from the SD card, if there is one. An old FILMS.TXT can point to labels or curves that no longer exist.
  2. Unpack the ZIP to the root of the SD card, so that DISKBOOT.BIN and the CHDK folder sit at the top level.
  3. Start EXCCD, either
    • once per power-on: switch on in playback mode, open MENU → Firm Update and confirm, or
    • automatically: make the card bootable (for example with STICK or CHDK's Make Card Bootable) and lock the card's write-protect switch. The camera still saves photos.
  4. Pick a look: in shooting mode press Print, flip through the labels, press FUNC/SET. The active look is shown at the bottom of the screen.

Updating later works the same way: delete the CHDK folder, unpack the new ZIP. The camera menus are currently in German.
